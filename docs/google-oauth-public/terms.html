What the software is

Daily Organiser bot is a private household log operated by Mykola Konovalov. It keeps household tasks, commitments, and a morning brief. It is not a chat. These public pages do not offer accounts, and they do not start a sign-in.

The household app at https://organiser.apidiscoverysolution.uk is not a link here. Opening it asks for a household access check. That check is not these terms, and it is not a Google sign-in.

Calendar use

Calendar use in the current code is read-only. The scopes are the two named in the privacy policy: viewing events, and seeing the calendar list. The brief shows today’s titles and times from the configured personal calendar. Settings can add or remove a calendar by name. The software does not create, edit, or delete Google Calendar events.

Google’s own terms apply to the Google account. This page does not change them.

Stopping the connection

The account holder can remove the app’s access from their Google Account. The privacy policy explains that this is not the same as deleting data the household app has already stored, and that this software has no Google disconnect button of its own.
