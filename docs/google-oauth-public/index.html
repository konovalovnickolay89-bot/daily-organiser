What the app does

Daily Organiser keeps household tasks, commitments, and a morning brief. With a personal Google calendar connected, that brief can list today’s appointment titles and times. The list is read from Google Calendar. The app does not create, edit, or delete those events.

Each line on the brief is the event title, or the word Busy when Google sends no title, and a local time or the words All day. The appointments section says: “Read only. This is not a calendar.”

There is no month grid and no public sign-up. These pages do not ask you to sign in, and they do not start a Google consent screen.

What Google Calendar is used for

The connection is read-only. The scopes named in the source are:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events.readonly — Google’s description: view events on all your calendars.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.calendarlist.readonly — Google’s description: see the list of Google calendars you’re subscribed to.

The intended calendar is the primary personal calendar. The software can also keep a calendar the operator adds by name in settings. Google’s events scope is worded more widely than “today on the primary calendar”. The privacy policy explains that difference.

This app does not run a Google sign-in callback. A client id, a client secret, and a refresh token are supplied from outside the app. The app uses that refresh token to ask Google for a short-lived access token, then reads the calendar.
